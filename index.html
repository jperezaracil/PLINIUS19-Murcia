What the model can do

Predicts

Heatwave days in years it never saw: test AUC 0.78–0.90 at 9 of 12 sites, above a trend-only model.

Detects

Most observed heatwave days are flagged at 8 of 12 sites (Libya 80 of 96, Croatia 149 of 216), but with more than two false alarms per hit overall.

Locates

Where the precursors are: warm air over the site and a Z500 ridge over or next to it.

Explains

How far ahead: local Tmax and a Z500 ridge mostly 3–20 days before the heatwave; remote SST only marks warm summers.

Framework

Conclusions

  1. 01STCO-FS skilfully predicts summer heatwave days at 9 of 12 Mediterranean sites (test AUC 0.78–0.90), beyond the warming trend.
  2. 02Skill is highest in the central and eastern Mediterranean; Murcia (0.65) and Algeria (0.60) show little predictability, and Tunisia adds nothing beyond the trend.
  3. 03Robust precursors are local and short-range: warm Tmax and a Z500 ridge over or next to the site, about 3–20 days ahead.
  4. 04The CRO selects all eight Z500 clusters somewhere, but only five of them (four regional modes) show a significant anomaly before heatwaves in both train and test; the Alps–Balkans ridge does so at five sites (Monaco, Croatia, Albania, Greece, Algeria).
  5. 05Dry soils are often selected 10–30 days ahead, but they are confirmed in 2011–2021 only at Turkey (3–14 d) and Tunisia (10–20 d), and only Turkey's signal survives detrending.
  6. 06The KMeans clusters behave as varimax-rotated EOFs: regional modes of variability.
